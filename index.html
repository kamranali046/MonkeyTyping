What is WPM?

Words per minute (WPM) measures how many words you can type in one minute. A "word" is standardized to five characters, so WPM = (correct characters / 5) / minutes. Raw WPM counts all typed characters including mistakes, while net WPM only counts correct ones. Accuracy is the percentage of correctly typed characters. Many users ask "wpm monkeytype typing test" or "typing monkey wpm test"; our wpm test reports both net and raw values like monkeytype typing.

Tips to improve typing speed

Focus on accuracy first; speed follows naturally. Keep your fingers on the home row, use all fingers, and avoid looking at the keyboard. Practice daily in short sessions and gradually increase difficulty. Aim for 95%+ accuracy before pushing for higher WPM. Monkey typing rewards consistency, so type monkey drills every day.
