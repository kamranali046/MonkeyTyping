Bubble Typing Game - Monkey Typing

A fun typing game where words and letters float upward in colored bubbles. Type the word shown on a bubble to burst it and earn points. The longer the word, the more points you score. Miss a bubble and you lose a life. Great for warming up and improving reaction time. If you enjoy monkeytype, this bubble game gives a playful twist to typing practice, mixing monkey typing speed with visual tracking.

How to play

Click start, then type the letters of any bubble. The game targets the bubble whose word matches what you type. Backspace corrects mistakes. Choose your mode (words, letters, or mixed), duration (30/60/120 seconds or endless) and difficulty (easy, hard, pro) before starting. You can also set a custom number of lives or unlimited. Monkey typer fans will recognize the familiar feel of monkeytype typing, but with bubbles.

Modes

  • Words: full words from the sentence pool, like a monkeytype typing test.
  • Letters: single letters for quick reflex training.
  • Mixed: a blend of words and letters.

Difficulty levels

  • Easy: slower bubbles, more lives (5).
  • Hard: balanced speed and 3 lives.
  • Pro: fast bubbles, 1 life.

Scoring

Each burst bubble awards points equal to its length times ten. Longer words and higher difficulties yield more points. Your best score is saved locally. Type monkey drills here to improve both speed and accuracy, a perfect companion to the typing test.

Why play

The bubble game trains quick recognition and typing accuracy in a playful way. It complements the typing test by adding speed pressure and visual tracking. High scores are saved locally in your browser.
