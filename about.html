About Us

Typing Test is a free, open, client-side typing practice platform. We built it to help people improve their typing speed and accuracy without sign-ups, paywalls, or distractions.

The typing test offers multiple modes (time, words, sentence), random sentences from a large pool, themes, and detailed statistics including WPM, raw WPM, accuracy and a WPM-over-time chart. The bubble typing game adds a playful way to practice quick recognition and typing under pressure.

Our mission

To make high-quality typing practice accessible to everyone, everywhere, for free. All processing happens in your browser; we do not store your keystrokes or personal data on any server.

Technology

The site is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. There is no backend, so the site is fast, private and reliable.
