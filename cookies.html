Cookie Policy

This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember information about your visit.

How we use cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Remembering your settings and preferences (theme, font size, mode).
  • Analytics to understand how visitors use the site.
  • Advertising, including personalized ads served by Google AdSense.

Third-party cookies

Advertising partners such as Google may place cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

More information

For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
